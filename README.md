# Serenity legal pages

Published legal pages for the **Serenity** Android app (`com.rajpar29.serenity`),
a parental control app that blocks keywords and apps on a single device.

- Privacy Policy: https://rajpar29.github.io/serenity-privacy/
- Terms of Service: https://rajpar29.github.io/serenity-privacy/terms.html

Serenity collects no personal data. Screen text read through Android's
AccessibilityService is matched against locally stored rules and discarded
immediately; the app holds no `INTERNET` permission and cannot transmit anything.

Contact: jayadityaparlia@gmail.com

This repository contains only the legal pages. The app source is kept separately.
