Serenity Privacy Policy

Applies to the Serenity Android app (com.rajpar29.serenity).
Effective 6 September 2026. Last updated 6 September 2026.

Serenity does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Serenity is a parental control app that blocks content and apps on a single device. It reads the text shown on screen in order to match the rules a parent has set. That text is checked and discarded immediately, on the device. The app has no internet permission, so it is not technically capable of sending anything anywhere.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Serenity Android app, referred to below as "Serenity", "we" or "us". Serenity is made and published by an independent individual developer based in India, not by a company. For any question about this policy or about the app, or to raise a privacy grievance, contact jayadityaparlia@gmail.com. That address is the single point of contact for all privacy matters.

What Serenity does

A parent or guardian sets up Serenity on a device and protects it with a PIN. They choose keywords and apps to block. When blocked content appears on screen, Serenity closes it by performing the system Back action and showing a short on-screen message.

Use of the Android AccessibilityService API

Serenity uses Android's AccessibilityService API. This is the only way an app can see what is displayed by other apps, which is what makes content blocking possible. Serenity is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities, and it does not declare itself as one.

What the service accesses

  • The text displayed on screen in other apps: titles, menu labels, messages, search terms and web page text.
  • The package name of the app currently shown.

What Serenity does with it

  • The text and package name are compared, in memory, with the blocking rules stored on the device.
  • If a rule matches, Serenity performs the Back action and displays a short message.
  • The text is then discarded. It is not written to a file, not added to a log, not kept in a database, and not shown to anyone.

What Serenity never does

  • It does not transmit screen content, or anything else, off the device. Serenity does not request the INTERNET permission, so it has no network access at all.
  • It does not record keystrokes, capture screenshots, or record audio or video.
  • It does not build a history or report of browsing, messages or app usage.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party SDK.
  • It does not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal information.

Before the accessibility service can be switched on, Serenity shows this same explanation inside the app and asks for explicit consent. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by turning the service off in Android Settings, under Accessibility.

Information stored on your device

Everything Serenity saves stays in the app's private storage on the device. None of it is sent anywhere, and none of it is accessible to us.

Device administrator permission

Serenity can optionally be granted the Android device administrator permission. It is used for one purpose only: so that the permission must be switched off before the app can be uninstalled, which stops the app being removed in a single step. Serenity requests no other device administrator abilities. It cannot lock the device, erase data, change passwords or control the camera. The permission is optional, and it can be revoked at any time in Android Settings, which also allows the app to be uninstalled normally.

Sharing and selling of data

Serenity collects no data, so there is no data to share or sell. We do not share information with third parties, advertisers or analytics providers. There are no third-party services embedded in the app. We have never sold or shared personal information, and we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising or profiling.

Your rights and choices

Because Serenity sends nothing off your device, we hold no personal information about you. That means there is no account to access, no profile to correct and no server-side record for us to delete. It also means we cannot identify you, and we could not link any data to you even if asked to.

You keep direct control over everything the app stores, at any time and without contacting us:

  • Review or change your rules and settings inside the app, behind your PIN.
  • Withdraw consent for screen reading by switching the accessibility service off in Android Settings, under Accessibility. Blocking stops immediately.
  • Delete everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Both permanently remove the rules, the settings and the PIN.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. These rights apply to personal data held by a controller. As we receive and hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to act on, and the controls listed above give you the same practical outcome directly. You always retain the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA and CPRA give you rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing. We collect none of the categories of personal information defined by those laws, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising a privacy right.

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal against a Data Fiduciary that processes your personal data. Serenity does not collect, receive or process any personal data, so no such data is held about you by us. If you nonetheless have a privacy concern, write to the address above and we will respond, and you retain your right to approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Serenity performs no automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and carries out no profiling. Because no data leaves the device, there are no international data transfers.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on the device, blocking rules and app settings may be included in the device backup that Android creates and stores under the device owner's own Google account, governed by Google's privacy policy rather than ours. We have no access to those backups. The encrypted PIN file is explicitly excluded from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Google Play

Serenity is distributed through Google Play. When you install or update an app, Google Play collects information about that transaction under Google's own privacy policy. That happens between you and Google. We receive no personal information from it, and we do not use any Play analytics or reporting service that would identify you.

Links that leave the app

The About screen can open this policy in your browser, or your email app to contact us. Once you follow one of those, you are in another app, governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Serenity does not fetch these pages itself, as it has no network access; it simply hands the address to the app you have already chosen.

Children's privacy

Serenity is a tool intended to be installed and configured by a parent, guardian or the device owner. It is not directed at children as its audience, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information about a child is ever gathered, stored remotely or disclosed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and in fact we collect none from any user.

Data retention and deletion

All of Serenity's data lives in the app's private storage on the device, for as long as the app is installed and no longer. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes the rules, the settings and the PIN. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side copy to request the deletion of. If device administrator is active, switch it off first so the app can be uninstalled.

Security

Screen text is processed in memory and discarded. The PIN is encrypted with a hardware-backed key from the Android Keystore. All app data is held in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app has no network capability, which removes the possibility of data being intercepted in transit. No method of protecting information is perfect, but because nothing is collected or transmitted, the exposure this app creates is limited to the device itself.
